# Translation rules: what was measured on Serum, and how it is used

This is the running record of facts measured on Serum 2 (with controlled probe patches, DawDreamer renders and the scripts in `calibration/`) and of the
rules the converter and engine apply because of them. It is written as dated notes, newest topics last, and is meant to be read before changing a rule.
File and function names refer to this repo. Where a note says "not carried" or "NOT fixed", that is still open.

## The standing rule

The aim is that every preset is faithful to its Serum settings, so that other Serum packs can be imported and recreated automatically. So: no per-preset
fits against a loss function (`HYBRID_DRY_FITS = False` in `deephouse2move.py`), and no special-casing a preset by name. Allowed: global unit constants measured
with controlled Serum probe patches (`calibration/probe_*.py`), and a per-preset loudness (volume) fit plus the limiter-share cap. No preset is exempt:
`Bass - A Classic` was once kept from a hand-tuned file and is now translated like the rest, as every future import will be.

**Why:** the end goal is a drop-in Serum pack importer; per-preset tuning does not transfer to new packs.
**How to apply:** fix translation bugs at the rule level and verify with a probe against Serum before touching a preset. Judge changes on many presets, not one:
the chain report's per-preset loss moves by +-4 between renders of the same state.

## Measured facts and rules
Facts measured on Serum that are not obvious from the code (all now in `deephouse2move.py`):
- Modulation source ids: Env1..Env4 = 2..5 (not 1..4), LFO1..10 = 6..15, Macro1..8 = 25..32.
- LFO curve y is stored down from the top (value = 1 - y); a segment bend c gives travel x**p with p from a measured table.
- A route moves its destination by amount x modulator value (bipolar: half that, either way): level (in the sqrt/knob domain), cutoff
  (cutoff-knob units), coarse pitch 128 st per 100 %.
- Compressor: Serum's Makeup is a linear factor (gain = 20 log10(makeup), 1 is unity), omitted params default to threshold 0.5, ratio 4,
  attack 90, release 90; its knee widens with threshold depth and an infinite ratio is a limiter (lower threshold, narrow knee). The
  engine's Comp stage (after distortion) takes its threshold as a pre-master level that the converter sets once from the closed-form volume.
- Moog-type ladder filters (MgL24, MgL18) show no resonance peak up to 40 %; the converter's old resonance law was far too high.
- Source routing: RoutingSlot0..4 = osc A, B, C, noise, sub; absent means osc A to filter, the rest bypass; `kRoutingDestDirect` bypasses
  too. The engine has per-source `*_post` (Direct) params. A pack table with the same file name as a factory one ("Basic Shapes.wav" in
  Deep House vs Analog/) must lose to the exact path under Serum's Tables folder (`resolve_table`). Sub `kRoundRect` is nearly a sine
  (harmonics below -33 dB), not a square. Effects after a Split node (branch_nodes) work on one band: not master width/reverb.
- Filter FX moved by an envelope: the engine's FX Filter stage (`fxf_*`, after the distortion, follows the loudest voice's filter
  envelope) matches Serum within ~1 harmonic (calibration/dev/probe_fxfilter_env.py). Modulator source 1 is not audible in a render.
- Filters with a Var knob (LH6) cannot be matched by any engine filter (7.5 dB off); `filter_model_key` looks up "MODEL@var" entries.
- Chorus/Flanger/Phaser are engine stages now (`ModFx` in fx.h, params `chor_*`, `flan_*`, `phas_*`, device page "Mod FX", converter
  `fxmap.modfx_params`; the old width fingerprint covers HyperD only). Measured on Serum: wet law as delay/reverb; chorus has 4 voices
  (2 per side), default Delay 5.0 ms / Delay2 ~5.2, default Filt is a 12 dB/oct LPF near 1 kHz, wet plateau about -3.5 dB; flanger Wet 100
  about -1.5 dB. NOT measured (guessed, marked in the notes): default Rate/Depth/Freq/Poles, depth-to-ms scale, flanger/phaser sweep
  ranges. Serum's noise osc can hold a one-shot click sample (short): left out. Macros that drive an effect's Wet are folded in
  (`fold_macro_wets`), 19 of 70 presets. `fit_level.py` (threaded) is unstable for some presets; `refit.py`-style sequential fit +
  `cap_limiter` is repeatable.
- Volume cap for the Deep House bank (2026-11-08): the converter's closed-form volume leaves some presets hotter than Serum's, and the engine's soft limiter
  (knee 0.7) then adds distortion Serum's render lacks (Keys - BUNT: EQ-only distortion floor about -47 dBc; volume 52 -> 40 takes its band loss 16.9 -> 7.8).
  `python calibration/dev/cap_bank.py SRC DEST` runs `import_pack.cap_limiter` (the Factory importer's rule: at most 1.5% of samples reach the shoulder) over a
  converted bank. Measured on all 70: mean band loss 12.955 -> 12.636, presets under 14 dB 42 -> 44; 17 volumes change, Can't Find You 19.4 -> 6.4,
  Keys - BUNT 19.8 -> 7.8, Nu Aspect 2 11.6 -> 9.3; Lead - Self Control 14.3 -> 16.8 and Keys - Why U So 6.5 -> 7.9 get worse (the lower level drifts from Serum's).
  A knee of 0.98 in the engine was worse than the cap.
- Noise slot holding a sample (2026-11-08): Serum plays it at its own octave spectrum, 11.6 dB under the sample's rms at volume 0.75 (White at 0.75 is -22.4 dB),
  `calibration/dev/probe_noise_sample.py`, 7 factory samples, bands within 1 dB. The converter used to read the sample name from the wrong node, so every such slot became
  white noise (Bass - Reiny: an envelope drove it to a +20 dB high-frequency floor). Now `sample_export.noise_standin` fits an engine noise (type, hp, lp, level) to the
  sample's octave levels. Measured on the 20 presets with such a slot: sum of band loss -7.1 (Reiny 18.7 -> 13.2, Hans Deeper 22.5 -> 17.3, 13 vs 12 under 14 dB),
  most others within +-1; 70-preset mean about -0.10. Narrow tonal samples (an organ, a balafon) are only roughly matched by a noise.
- Wavetable position is STEPPED, not blended, for tables whose WAV `clm ` chunk reads "<!>2048 0..." (frame = floor(pos*N); Default Shapes,
  Basic Shapes, DS Saw and Tri); "2..." tables interpolate. The engine reads the flag (`Wavetable::stepped`); tablor_cli.cpp loads tables
  exactly like the module (0.9-peak normalise + stepped) so level fits are valid. Modulation source ids: 1 = Mod Wheel (0 in every render).
- Serum's own window can be captured: `calibration/dev/ui_shot.py "Preset" out.png --click "x,y;x,y,notches" --crop ... --scale ...` (DawDreamer
  open_editor; pops a window for ~10 s; tabs at window px osc 205,78 mix 275,78 fx 343,78 matrix 410,78). The title shows "Init" but
  the panels are the preset's. Crops at scale 0.6 are cheap.
- Sample oscillator (Sample/Multisample, 8 of 70 DH presets): `sample_export.py` turns the SFZ/audio into a .tbs set under
  out/move/data/UserData/UserLibrary/Samples/<bank>/ (Serum's own sounds: never commit or share; deploy with deploy_samples.sh);
  engine `sample.h` + params `smp_*`; the osc's Loop Start/End % override the SFZ loops; sample osc warps (HPF etc.), unison, own env
  release are not modelled. Level vs Serum varies by sample (-7..+3 dB), left at 1.0.
- FX audit: `calibration/dev/fx_audit.py` (one effect at a time, Serum vs engine, on noise; run it after ANY effect-mapping change; covers Delay,
  Reverb, Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, EQ(+static Filter FX), HyperD; not yet Distortion, Comp, dynamic Filter FX). Its stereo check compares
  correlation change only (Serum's noise source has a random L/R level imbalance). Found: delay time is a STEPPED tempo-synced position
  (edges ~0.032/0.063/0.105/0.185 -> 1/16,1/8,1/4,1/2,1 bar at 120 BPM; OffsetL/R multiply; unwritten = 1/4); delay mix = stored Wet through
  the smoothstep (the fingerprint echo ratio failed whenever the assumed echo time was wrong); the delay's Freq/BW band-pass acts on the
  first echo (engine had it on repeats only; fitted DELAY_BW_SCALE 2.0, kDelayOut 1.17). 6 dB/oct Filter FX = fitted hishelf -24 dB.
  Remaining: reverb pre-delay/tail shapes, Filter FX drive and resonance, 2 delays (Deep In The Water, NOTSOBAD).
- Other fixes of 2026-11-03: multiband comp upward boost capped at 12 dB (it ran away in silent tails: first note after a pause came out
  loud); filter cutoff stepped in 4 pieces per block (a one-sample click at note start); measured BEND_POT and FILTER_DRIVE_POT tables
  (the straight lines were 3x too strong); output dither; compressor thresholds follow the fitted volume (calibration/comp_rescale.py);
  the deploy must also upload wavetables (deploy_wavetables.sh) or presets name files the Move lacks.
- Filter FX measured on Serum (calibration/dev/probe_fxfilter_drive.py, probe_fxfilter_types.py [--hp]): an unwritten Reso plays as 0 (not 10);
  every LP/HP ladder type is that many identical one-pole filters at the STATED cutoff (MgL24/L24 4, MgL18/L18 3, MgL12/L12 2, MgL6/L6/default 1;
  H24/H18/H12/H6 likewise; DirtyMg/LadderAcid/ZDF_A taken as 3, unmeasured); two poles = a Q 0.5 biquad, an odd pole is fitted by a shelf. Default type
  with Reso lowers the passband (-2.8 dB at 10, -7.3 at 35, -14 at 100) and puts a peak at the cutoff (table in fxfilter_drive_probe2.json,
  `fxmap.fx_filter_reso`); Drive is a level-independent gain (+3.2 at 10, +7.7 at 25, +14 at 50, +23.5 at 100). The engine EQ keeps two LPs/HPs in a
  row (`reduce_bands`). EQ audit 50/61 -> 58/61 with these (the audit now ignores an all-over EQ gain: the loudness is fitted afterwards).
- Reverb: the audit's tail time is wet-dependent, so it compares the slope rt60 now (all of a preset's reverbs together). The old reverb tail
  colour read a fixed 0.3-1.3 s window: for short reverbs that is a numerical floor (white), so it read "bright", the bisect hit damp 20 and the engine
  tail came out ~30 dB too quiet (Rivo, Reiny, Atlantic...). `fxmetrics.tail_window` ends the window where the tail has fallen 35 dB; 23 short-tail
  fingerprints were re-measured. The chain report's >8k / 3-8k errors are mostly tiny absolute levels (-60..-130 dBFS): do not chase them as audible.
- Pads with a SECOND voice filter (MIND: filter2_enabled) are far too bright in the engine (1-3 kHz +18 dB): the second filter is not built.
- SECOND VOICE FILTER (built 2026-11-03, 15 of 70 presets use it): engine `flt2_*` pots + per-source balance `wt1_bal..smp_bal` (0 = Filter 1 only,
  the default; 127 = Filter 2 only), cutoff destination "Filter 2 Freq" (MD_FLT2_FREQ, ME_DST 23), Filter 2 follows the one filter envelope by its own
  `flt2_env`. Serum's FilterBalance (percent, 0 = middle, UNWRITTEN = -100 = Filter 1 only): Filter 1 gets (1-t)/2 and Filter 2 (1+t)/2 of the source
  (calibration/dev/probe_filter2.py). A disabled filter passes its share unfiltered; a source in `kRoutingDestFilter` only. Unsupported F2 models (formant,
  comb, Diffuser) are left open. Modulation routes carry `dest_id` 0/1 for the two filters. An UNWRITTEN voice-filter Type is a 12 dB/oct low-pass
  (MgL12-like), not MgL24 as the converter had assumed (11 presets): `DEFAULT_VOICE_FILTER`.
- NOISE OSCILLATOR (2026-11-04, probe_noise_color.py, noise_survey.py; supersedes the older "unwritten = pink through a 950 Hz high-pass", which was Bass - Gravity's
  Pink / Color 0.75 left in the probe base's NoiseOsc3): the noise osc has Type (White / Pink / Brown / Geiger, unwritten = a noise flat to 1.6 kHz rising 3-5 dB/oct above)
  and Color (0.5 neutral). Color is two 12 dB/oct Butterworth filters: below 0.5 a LOW-pass with corner 460 Hz at 0.2 and x4.5 per 0.1; above 0.5 a HIGH-pass, corner
  460 Hz at 0.7 (0.6: 100 Hz, 0.8: 2 kHz); 0.9+ adds a rising tilt (not carried), 1.0 is silent. Engine: noise_type 0 White (gain x3.05 so it is 4.1 dB over Pink like
  Serum), 1 Pink, 4 Brown (leaky integrator, corner 90 Hz, 0.6 dB over Pink), 2/3 legacy Pink HP 950/1190; new pots noise_hp (0 off) / noise_lp (127 off), 20 Hz..20 kHz
  log. Converter: `noise_color_pots`, `NOISE_TYPE_ENGINE`; the unwritten type is carried as White minus 2.2 dB (shape rms 4-8 dB, LF too low). A noise SAMPLE
  (relativePathToNoiseSample) keeps `noise_type_for`. Self Control Noise 29 -> 19 loss, Can't Find You 11 -> 6, Keys Other Side 18 -> 12. One-shot noise (kParamOneShot) and
  Geiger are not carried.
- Voice-filter DRIVE in Serum is a plain gain (+3.3 dB at 10 %, +7.6 at 25, +12.5 at 50; a loud saw tops out near +13, a quiet noise reaches +24), every
  harmonic by the same dB; the engine's tanh Drive adds less gain and some harmonics. Kept: the engine pot up to its 25 % value (Bass - AVAION's 320 Hz band
  needs that bit of saturation) plus a gain stage after each filter (`flt_gain`, `flt2_gain`, 0..24 dB; `FILTER_DRIVE_MAKEUP_DB`). Checked end to end
  against Serum with calibration/dev/check_filter2.py (8 synthetic cases, rms 0.6-3.5 dB on harmonics).
- The Distortion effect has its own 12 dB state-variable filter (Freq, BW = resonance, LPHP 0 = low-pass .. 100 = high-pass; PrePost 1 = AFTER the
  distortion, 2 = BEFORE, 0/unwritten off). Carried as an EQ band only when it is after (`fxmap.dist_filter_bands`: Origins, all things break, Movement);
  a filter before the distortion (AVAION: LP 144 Hz) cannot be an EQ band (the harmonics the overdrive makes sit above it), so it is not carried.
  Distortion modes/other params still unaudited; Distortion LevelOut (-6 dB unwritten, -15 at 0.3) is not carried (uniform: the loudness fit).
- The chain report's 3-8k and >8k band errors vary by 10 dB from run to run on the same state (random unison phases, aliasing at -75 dB): inaudible,
  never act on them; judge by audited effects and the 80 Hz-3 kHz bands.
- A route's SECOND source (ModSlot `source` = [main, aux]) scales its depth: a macro as aux multiplies it by the macro value (MIND's filter envelope is gated
  by macro 1 = 0, so Serum's filter stays at its base cutoff while the engine opened it; fixed in `physical`). Aux sources 16-21 (velocity-like) are not carried.
- Staged 2026-11-03 night (not installed): calibration/g15_final = g39 (all 70 refitted, mean chain loss 15.97, level |err| 0.89 dB; previous staged set in
  calibration/g15_before_f2), ARM dsp.so md5 24066037809d8edd40f914d37e4289fc (second filter, noise types, filter gain stage). A full refit takes ~25 min
  when nothing else runs (it takes 100 min with Serum/other jobs competing). Voice-section survey (calibration/voice_survey.py) is unreliable at high
  frequencies: the dither floor of a quiet engine render and Serum's digital silence read as 50-80 dB differences.
- g15 set (calibration/g15_final, staged in out/, not installed): mean loss 16.93 vs 17.69 for g14; cycle script calibration/run_g15b.sh
  (fit_level only refits names removed from level_fits.json; level fits are keyed by params_sig).

- DISTORTION (2026-11-04, `fx.h processDistCal`, params `dist_cal`/`dist_out`/`dist_level`, `fxmap.dist_params`, curves in calibration/dist_curves.json from
  probe_dist_transfer.py + calibrate_dist2.py, stage audit calibration/dev/stage_audit.py). Serum's Wet is a SMOOTHSTEP s with the dry part (1 - s^2):
  out = LevelOut_gain * ((1 - s^2) x + s K f(G x)); LevelOut unwritten = 0.5 and its gain is (LevelOut / 0.5)^2 on the SUM (dry too). The curve is level-
  dependent (the old fit matched harmonics at one input level only): tanh / biased tanh (bias 0.003) / hard clip / sine (SineShaper, a folder) with G and K
  per mode and drive. Measured at the probe preset's default master, so the engine pots follow G x probe gain (10^(master_default_db/20)); the stage sits
  BEFORE the engine master gain, so `comp_rescale.rescale` moves dist_drive (+dB) and dist_out (-dB) with the final volume, like the comp thresholds.
  Modes mapped: default/SoftClip/TapeSat tanh, Asym/Diode2 biased, Overdrive/HardClip hard clip, SineShaper sine. Poor fits remain: Diode2 (5-8 dB), Asym at
  high drive (4-5), SineShaper (3-8; Rivo). Only the FIRST master Distortion is carried (SWEDISHMF and The Selected have a second).
- COMP thresholds were measured at the default master; Serum's master comes AFTER the FX, so thresholds in output dB shift by 20 log10(master / default
  master) (`master_vs_default_db`; Reiny master 1.0 = +11.8 dB). Before this the engine over-compressed every high-master preset (Reiny -11 dB vs Serum +3).
- `import_pack.finalize_level(m, it, serum, bank)` is the importer's level step. The comp thresholds and distortion gains work BEFORE the master gain, so
  their reference level is the volume at which the engine's VOICE (fx_on 0) matches Serum's voice-only render (`voice_level_error_db`, clamped +-12 dB), set
  once with `comp_rescale.rescale`; the loudness fit then moves the volume AFTER them (`fit_volume(follow=False)`). Following the fitted volume instead
  (what the scratch refit did before) was wrong whenever the full-chain fit deviated from the voice level for other reasons (Other Side: +12 dB because
  of effects the engine orders differently; its comp then over-compressed by 8.5 dB). Comp stage level error on the audit: 2.85 -> 1.83 dB mean.
  Chain loss is flat (15.8) because its per-preset noise is +-4; use the stage audits. a sequential refit script that calls finalize_level is the repeatable way to refit.
- Serum's route aux curve (`kParamAuxCurveData` on the slot) changes what an aux 16/17 does: Tonic Walter's aux-16 route at velocity 100 is the UNSCALED
  route (60 darker, 127 brighter); a static 100/127 scale made it 25 dB too dark. Aux 16/17 are left at full depth (tried and reverted).
- Serum's reverb Delay (pre-delay) param has NO effect on the wet timing at wet 100 (hall; 0 / 104 / 300 ms identical); do not add a pre-delay.
- Aux sources of a route (probe_aux_source.py, plain slot): 16 = velocity and 17 = note number scale the depth by x/127; 18, 19, 24 are closed at rest,
  20 and 23 constant, 21 and 22 random per note. None carried (see the aux curve note).
- Renders of the same engine patch vary by ~3 dB in level from run to run (random unison phases): average 3+ renders before drawing a level conclusion.
- The engine voice level vs Serum's at the closed-form master: median |diff| 3.6 dB, scatter +-8 dB (calibration/dev/voice_level.py); the loudness fit moves
  the volume by up to +-20 dB from the closed form, mostly because the FX chain (comp, EQ) differs, so absolute stage levels depend on the rescale.
- Dynamic Filter FX audit (stage_audit.py --kinds Filter) is dominated by noise floors at HF (Serum voice has a flat -45 dB floor); not informative.
- Ping-pong delay (calibration/dev/probe_pingpong.py): structure matches (L at T, R at 2T, L at 3T, -6 dB per echo at fb 50 %), but NOTSOBAD's narrow band-pass
  delay (BW 0.75) loses ~3.6 dB per pass in the engine's 1-pole HP+LP vs Serum's ~2.4 (decay rt30 0.37 vs 0.9 s); Sleepless's delay first echo is 5.7 dB
  too loud (the engine's 6 dB/oct skirt vs Serum's steeper one). Not fixed.

- DELAY TEMPO SYNC (2026-11-04): Serum's delay times are note steps (62.5 ms .. 2 s at 120 BPM). Engine `dly_sync` On scales dly_time / dly_timer by 120 / host BPM
  (Schwung's get_bpm, clamped 40..300; refreshFx re-reads it); the converter writes dly_sync=1 whenever a delay is carried. The CLI renders at the default 120 BPM, so
  every audit and loss number is unchanged by it. Test: tests/test_engine.cpp testDelaySync.
- The noise oscillator is mono with a RANDOM per-note L/R level imbalance in Serum (corr 1.000, side/mid -13..-29 dB, mean |L-R| 2.3 dB); the engine now draws a pan
  of +-0.27 per note (`noisePan`). Without it noise-only presets had a side/mid error of 65 dB in the chain loss (Self Control Noise, Keys - Loop).
- The chain loss's side/mid term (0.25 x |dB error|, no floor) is dominated by cases where BOTH sides are nearly mono (-30 vs -80 dB: oskar med k, InDeep); not audible,
  do not chase it. The effect audit's delay "first echo" check only runs when an echo is found above -35 dB on the engine side, so some old OKs were unchecked.

- AUX GATES (2026-11-04): a route whose second source is the mod wheel (1) or 18/19/24 (closed at rest) is OFF in a static preset (amount 0); 21/22 (random per note) take
  half the depth. The converter used to carry the LFO route in full: Bass - The Selected (an LFO gated by the mod wheel into the filter) went 24 -> 11 loss, Keys - Billboard
  (a vibrato gated by a random aux) 17 -> 10.5. A MAIN-source random route (21/22 straight to table position etc.) folded at its mean did NOT help (Atlantic, Ari worse): left alone.

- Routes into an envelope's SUSTAIN (macro, velocity, note) are folded into the stored sustain (Bass - Let It Go: velocity +73 % into the filter envelope held its filter open;
  loss 40.7 -> 37.4). Attack / decay / release are log time knobs and are not folded. A route with an envelope or LFO as the aux gate takes half depth (two vibrato routes:
  Self Control 25.6 -> 21.5). Chain-report loss moves by up to +-5 on presets with HF energy near the floor (Keys - BUNT) for a 3-pot change: judge a change on several presets.

- VELOCITY -> AMPLITUDE (2026-11-04): Serum's loudness is flat in velocity unless a route (source 16 -> Global Voice Amp) puts it there; with the route it is linear
  (20 log10(vel/127)). The engine's original law (v 25^v 0.04, params vca_vel default 100) made every converted preset follow velocity steeply while Serum's did not

- DELAY FILTER (2026-11-04, probe_delay_filter2.py, third-octave response of 8 Freq/BW pairs): a 1-pole high-pass + 1-pole low-pass (6 dB/oct) with UNITY gain at the centre;
  corners at Freq x 2^(+-0.8 BW) (DELAY_BW_SCALE 1.6; 2.0 was the older coarse fit). The engine's pair lost R/(1+R) at the centre (2.6 dB per pass at BW 0.75, so narrow
  echoes died faster than Serum's); fx.h processDelay now applies the make-up (1 + R) / R. Delay audit 32/35 (31 before, 33 at the start of the day, one of whose OKs was an
  unchecked missing echo). NOTSOBAD (ping-pong, BW 0.75) first echo -16.5 Serum vs -22.7 engine (was -25.0 / none found); its second delay node is still not carried.
- Another DC fix: the calibrated distortion removes the DC of its whole output (8 Hz one-pole; Bass - Rivo / Gravity / NOTSOBAD had 0.02-0.08 DC at soft velocity).

- REVERB on played notes (calibration/dev/reverb_stage_audit.py, reverb_tail_fit_probe.json; PARTLY FIXED 2026-11-04: wet gain kRevOut 1.50 -> 2.25, tail medians now -0.9 / -2.8 / -2.1 dB re Serum in the three windows instead of -1.9 / -4.5 / -3.7, sustain add +0.2 dB; line length scaling x1.6 / x2.4 did not help; 3.0 centres the tails but makes steady noise 5.5 dB hot; measured with calibration/dev/reverb_engine_only.py; the demo-MIDI level of Other Side / Can't Find You / Let It Go / Self Control did not change, so their quietness is not the reverb): the engine's reverb tail is a median 5 dB under Serum's on real notes
  (up to -20 on bass / long pads), although the effect audit (noise burst, rt60 slope) says OK. On a wet-100 burst the two envelopes have the SAME late slope but Serum's
  holds its peak 50-100 ms longer and decays more gently right after it (MIND: Serum -2 -3 -4 -5 dB at 240-400 ms, engine -3 -5 -7 -9; Lynnic and HotLap 4-5 dB), and on a few

## Distortion Overdrive: a level-dependent gain, and the AVAION diagnosis corrected
Measured with `calibration/dev/probe_dist_curve.py` (a sine rendered with and without the effect, output against input sample by sample, the voice's random start phase
aligned on the fundamental) and `probe_dist_od.py` (harmonics 1-15); the gain table is `calibration/dist_overdrive_gain.json`.
- Serum's HardClip and SoftClip are static curves (small-signal gain the same at input peaks of 0.035 and 0.35; HardClip fits a hard clip to 0.5-0.9 dB rms over harmonics
  1-9). The engine's hard clip is right for HardClip.
- Overdrive is NOT static: its output is a hard ceiling at 0.386 (probe units) for every drive, and its gain depends on the LEVEL of the input, not on the instant:
  at drive 100 the initial slope is 33x for an input peak of 0.01, 15x at 0.05, 6x at 0.14, 2.7x at 0.35; at drive 60 it is 5.5x up to a peak of 0.09 and falls to 1.85x at 0.35;
  drive 20 is linear (0.91x) at every level. Above the drive's own maximum gain the gain falls so that gain x peak creeps up only slowly (drive 100: 0.76 -> 0.95 over a 10x
  level range), i.e. a leveller (about 5:1 above a knee) ahead of the clip. So the clip is never driven harder than about 2x its ceiling, which is why its harmonics fall
  faster than a hard clip's (3rd -12 dB, 5th -21, 7th -36 re the fundamental at drive 100). The engine's `dist_curves.json` fit (one G, K per drive) is the compromise over four
  levels (Overdrive fit error 2-5 dB at drives 50-70, HardClip 0.1). The attack and release of the leveller are NOT measured (a clipped output hides the gain), so it is not modelled yet.
  33 of the 626 factory presets have an Overdrive; 1 of the 70 Deep House presets (Bass - AVAION, drive 25, which is in the linear zone).
- CORRECTION to the Bass - AVAION note above: the engine's hard-clip harmonics are NOT why it got worse after the pre-filter. At drive 25 the Overdrive is in its linear zone,
  and engine bands above 640 Hz are the same with the distortion bypassed (dist_mix 0), with dist_drive 10 or 20, and with the pre-filter off. The excess lives in bands
  at -75 to -90 dB re the peak (the engine's 16-bit dither/aliasing floor against Serum's digital silence); the pre-filter only lowers the level fit's headroom a few dB
  there (volume 45 -> 53). The 8.6 -> 16.2 is the metric reading below-audible bands, not a clipper problem. In the 100-1000 Hz bands the pre-filter is mixed (one band 12 dB too low, another 6 dB closer), so the pre-filter stays on structure, not on this preset's score.

## Oscillator warps: the Dist* waveshapers (2026-11-08 night)
Of the 59 factory presets whose only blocker is an oscillator warp, 27 use a Dist* waveshaper (Dist Tube 14, SoftSat 6, TapeSat 3, Diode 2 2, Stomp Box 1, Diode 1 1), the next block FM / PD / RM from the Sub, Noise or another oscillator (about 30, not done).
- **Measured** (`calibration/dev/probe_warp_dist.py`, a pure sine oscillator through each warp at amounts 0..1, harmonics 1..12): every Dist* warp is a STATIC odd shaper of the waveform (even harmonics at the floor) except Diode 2, which is asymmetric (even and odd
  harmonics) and is left unsupported (2 presets). The fundamental gains a little with the amount; Dist Tube's 3rd harmonic is -36 dB re the fundamental at 0.1, -20 at 0.5, -11 at 1.0; Diode 1 is nearly a hard clip from 0.1.
- **Fitted** (`calibration/dev/fit_warp_dist.py` -> `warp_dist_curves.json`): out = K f(G x) of a unit sine, f chosen per mode from hard clip / softsign / cubic soft clip over the odd harmonics 3..11, G and K per amount. Dist Tube = softsign (0.8 dB rms), SoftSat = cubic
  (1.6), TapeSat = softsign (2.9), Diode 1 = cubic (0.8), Stomp Box = hard clip (1.7). tanh was left out on purpose: `std::tanh` cost 7x the other curves per stream-sample, and nothing fitted notably better with it.
- **Engine**: `wtN_wshape` (0 off, 2 clip, 3 softsign, 4 cubic) with `wtN_sgain` (G = 0.02 x 15000^(pot/127)) and `wtN_sout` (K = 0.5 x 120^(pot/127)) per oscillator, applied to every unison stream's sample before the stack's gains (`WTOscillator::postProcess`), as Serum applies a
  warp inside the oscillator. The converter (`warp_shaper_pots`) interpolates G and K in the log between the measured amounts and writes the keys only for oscillators that use one (so no other preset's state, or its level-fit signature, changes). A second warp on the same oscillator
  is still not carried. Native test `testWarpShaper`.
- **Cost** (native x86, `testWarpShaper`, 16 unison streams, one second of audio): 3.7 ms with no shaper, 4.9-5.2 ms with one (+30-40 % of the oscillator, only on the oscillators that use it); about 0.4 % of a core without it. The ARM figure is not measured (the Move is offline).
- **The filter, FM, PD and RM warps** (one more warp per oscillator: `wtN_xw` 0 off, 1 Filter LP, 2 Filter HP, 3 FM Sub, 4 PD Sub, 5 RM Sub, with `wtN_xa` the Serum amount in thousandths; laws in `wt_oscillator.h`, native test `testXwarp`, the converter's `XWARP_MODES`). A second warp on the same oscillator is carried (`wtN_xw2`, `xa2`, `xs2`; applied after the first), and so are the FM / PD / RM / AM warps driven by another oscillator (`wtN_xs` 0 Sub, 1..3 oscillator A..C; see the next paragraph). The warps driven by the noise or by Filter 1 are not. What was measured (`calibration/dev/probe_warp_fm.py`: a sine oscillator, a Sub sine a fifth above, Sub level 0 so only the warp is heard; the sidebands at fc + n fm read as Bessel lines):
  - **FM from Sub** is a frequency modulation with a fixed deviation in Hz, D = 58 200 a^4 Hz (index D / fm; at the note an octave up with the Sub tracking it the index halves, and with only the Sub an octave up it halves too: it follows the Sub's pitch, not the carrier's). **PD from Sub** is a phase modulation of index 33.6 a^4 rad at any pitch. **RM from Sub** is (1 - a) x + a 1.314 x S: the carrier falls as (1 - a) and each sideband is 0.657 a. The Sub is taken at unit level whatever its own level, so the engine renders it once per block when a warp needs it and the audible Sub is that signal scaled.
  - **An oscillator as the modulator** (`calibration/dev/probe_warp_osc.py`, same sine-and-fifth probe with a second wavetable oscillator at level 0): the menu's "Osc" is the first of the OTHER two oscillators (A for B and C, B for A), "Osc 2" the second (C for A and B, B for C); `FM_OSC`, `FM_OSC2`, `PD_OSC`, `PD_OSC2`, `RM_OSC`, `RM_OSC2`, `AM_OSC`. A modulator that is switched OFF gives no modulation at all (the converter leaves the warp out). The modulator is taken at unit level whatever its own level, and it is 1.52 times as strong as the Sub at the same amount: FM deviation 90 000 a^4 Hz (the Sub's is 58 200), PD index 51 a^4 rad (33.6), RM (1 - a) x + a 2.0 x S (sidebands a, the Sub's 0.657 a), AM x (1 + a 2.0 S). The engine renders each needed oscillator once per block as a single voice (its table, position, pitch, sync and bend, none of its own warps). `PD_OSC` used to be carried as a Bend stand-in; it is a phase modulation from the other oscillator, so it now uses this stage. `testXwarp` checks FM, PD, RM, AM and two warps in turn against these numbers.
  - **Filter LP / HP**: a first-order filter with an ABSOLUTE corner (`probe_filterwarp.py`: log2 Hz at amount 0 .. 1 = 5.43, 5.91, 6.41, 7.39, 8.37, 9.37, 10.35, 11.35, 12.32, 13.37, 14.57, 17.56 at 0, .05, .1, .2 ... .9, 1); LP is open at 1, HP shut. The engine runs one trapezoidal one-pole over the whole unison stack.
  - Against Serum's measured lines the engine's FM, PD and RM come within 0.02 of the carrier-relative level at the first sidebands (`testXwarp`). A saw Sub is not covered: a saw Sub shifts the carrier off its pitch in Serum (it has a DC offset as a modulator) and the lines were not read there. Cost: one sample-by-sample pass on an oscillator that uses a PD / FM warp; the filter and RM warps are one multiply or one filter per sample of the stack.

## Effects the engine has no stage for: ask Serum whether they are audible (2026-11-09)

- **Ceilings first.** Before building a Split-branch stage or a frequency shifter, the converter was told to ignore the effect (temporary switch, not kept) and the render gate run on the presets it blocks: Bode 4 of 12 would pass (the other 8 fail for other reasons), Split with branch effects 1 of 11, Filter FX 19 of 29. The earlier estimates (about 9 for Split, 12 for Bode) were the converter's count, not what the gate keeps; Split and Bode are worth about 5 presets at most.
- **Rule** (`import_pack.py`, `ignored_effects_off`, `effects_inaudible`): when the only thing besides a missing fingerprint that stops a preset is effects the converter leaves out (Filter FX with a model or modulator it cannot carry, a Bode, a Split and its branch modules, a Comp / Distortion / Utils it has no stage for), Serum renders the preset a second time with exactly those nodes switched off. The effects count as inaudible when the octave-band gap between the two (the gate's own measure) is no more than Serum's render-to-render gap for the same preset (the two full renders against each other) plus 1.5 dB, and the level gap is within 1 dB. Otherwise the preset stays blocked and the reason says what Serum showed. A Distortion's own filter, bus-rack effects and every other reason are not eligible. `--no-ab-ignore` turns it off.
- **Result on the factory set** (79 effect-only presets tested, the final run with the cache cleared for the bank name): 10 pass the A/B test, 8 of them pass the gate and were added (`out_import_v5/Factory`: 108 -> 116). Hands Up (bands 4.9 against noise 2.6 on the rerun, 3.8 against 3.2 the first time) and Piano Space (level +1.0 dB) sit on the margin and are out; the test is noisy within about 1 dB of its limit.
- **What the test cannot see.** It is the octave-band spectrum over the first 1.2 s, so it is blind to phase-only effects (a Diffuser, a comb tuned above the bands, a slow frequency shift of a few Hz). The presets it passes carry such a filter in Serum and not in the engine (Modular: Diffuser; Artform, Delight, Bright Octaves: CombP; Analog Soundtrack: Bode, Shift about 1 Hz). They are noted in the import log ("left out, inaudible in Serum") and should be auditioned.
- **Bode, measured** (`calibration/dev/probe_bode.py`, `probe_bode_b.json`; a 220 Hz sine through one Bode at Wet 100): the node has two outputs, the input shifted down and up by Shift/100 x Range Hz (Shift +-100 %, Range in Hz as stored: Range 100, Shift 100 gives 120 and 320 Hz; default Range about 22). Feedback, Blur, DelayTime and DelayBalance are not probed. Not built: only about 4 factory presets would gain.

## Added 2026-11-09 (night): the Clip Player (SEQ presets that play a note pattern)
- Serum 2's Clip Player plays a stored MIDI clip when a key goes down. ## Added 2026-11-09 (night 2): the arpeggiator
Serum 2's arpeggiator (`Arp0.kParamEnabled`; 33 of the 626 factory presets) is carried as the state key `arp=shape,step,gate,trRange,trShift,transpose,wrapRange,wrapSemis,zoneMin,zoneMax,len|tick,dur,idx,vel|...` (`deephouse2move.arp_from_tree`, `Engine::arp*`). Everything below was measured with the Serum plugin and a sine patch, keys held as chords, pitch read every 4-125 ms (`arplib.py`-style probes, scratch):
- **The active clip** is `ArpClip<kParamActiveClipID>` (default 0); its `plainParams` hold the settings, and its `clip` holds the pattern (the node needs a `clip` key, even empty, or the shape is ignored).
- **Shapes** (`kParamShape`, default Up): Up and Down walk the held keys by pitch, UpDown turns round without repeating the ends, Played is the order the keys were pressed, Pattern plays the clip, RandNoDup / RandOnce / RandDrift are random (carried as random without an immediate repeat, and plain random). A key outside `Arp0` KeyZoneMin..Max is played as a plain note.
- **Rate** (`kParamRate`, default 1/16, 0.125 s at 120 BPM): quantised into bands 0.115 wide, each half the length of the one below (1/16 at 0.4425..0.5575, 1/32 above, 1/8 below). A Pattern clip has its own bands, 0.07 wide (`ARP_PATTERN_EDGES`), and its 1/16 cells are stretched to the step. With Beat Sync off the rate is free: 1 Hz at 0, 10 Hz at 0.5, 100 Hz at 0.9 (log10 Hz = 1.72 r + 0.56 r^2); the engine holds it at 120 BPM. Dotted and Triplets move the bands (Dotted: 3/4 of the plain step in some, plain in others) and are not applied; the three presets that set Dotted land on the plain 1/16 grid.
- **Gate** (`kParamGate`, default 100): the note's length as a percent of the step (of the clip note for Pattern); above 100 the notes overlap. **Transpose** adds semitones. **TransposeRange** repeats the held keys that many more times, each **TransposeShift** semitones higher (default 0, so a range without a shift changes nothing).
- **Pattern**: the clip's loop is `regionEndBeats`, else 4 beats; a note's `noteNum` is an index into the held keys sorted by pitch (negative counts from the top); an index past the keys wraps round them, and each wrap adds **WrapTranspose** semitones (default 12) up to **WrapRange** wraps (a negative wrap goes down; the count runs 0..WrapRange then starts over). A held note is joined at the arp's own beat grid in Serum; the engine starts the clock at the first key down.
- Not carried: Phantom wrap mode, step actions (Chord), retrig, velocity decay, repeats, chance. The importer's gate decides what is kept.
- Result: of the 33, 8 pass the gate (ARP - Altar, Legend Epix, Rainfall, LD - In Sphere, BA - Harmonic Twist, SEQ - Fifth DMension, Running down the Hill, Stressed Acid); the rest are stopped by other gaps: MultiSample / Granular oscillators (12), FM / RM / Dist warps (7), Bode and Split (5), a convolution reverb. ARP - Acid101 passes the level but not the bands (20.7 dB; its Serum render holds a steady level between steps that the engine's does not, undiagnosed).

## Macros (2026-11-10)

- The converter still folds every macro at its saved position (`fold`), so a preset loads sounding as Serum's does. On top of that each macro a preset routes (a mod route whose source or aux source is 25..32) is mapped a SECOND time with that macro at the far end of its range (100 % if it is saved below 50 %, else 0): `deephouse2move.attach_macros`. The integer pots that differ become that macro's routes, as the pot's travel over the macro's whole range (`macro_travel`). Linear between the two mappings, so a law that bends (an oscillator volume, a cutoff) is followed on average.
- Only pots on the `MACRO_POT` list are routed (levels, positions, tune, detune, filter, envelopes, effect mixes and times, EQ bands and so on). `volume` is never routed (its closed form would chase the oscillator levels). A macro that changes a switch or a choice (distortion mode, an EQ band's type, which wavetable an oscillator holds, so an oscillator's level sorting it into another slot) is left at its saved position, with a note ("is left at its saved position"; the word "not carried" is avoided because `unsupported_reasons` counts it). 34 of 305 macros in the Deep House pack.

The engine moves each routed pot by travel x (the macro's change) / 127 when a macro pot is set (`Engine::macroMoved`); relative, so editing a routed pot by hand and then turning the macro keeps the edit. Pots stop at their own range, which loses the overshoot (a macro pushed past a pot's end does not come back by the same amount).
- Check: `calibration/dev/macro_check.py` renders each macro at its far end in Serum and in the engine (`TB_MACRO="k=pot"` in tablor_cli) and compares the CHANGE in third-octave bands. ## Added 2026-11-13: Split / SplitMS band stage, envelope- and LFO-moved Distortion Wet

- **Serum's Split is a 4th-order Linkwitz-Riley crossover** (`calibration/dev/probe_split_band.py`: noise through a Split at 250 and 1000 Hz with a muted Distortion, Wet 100 and LevelOut 0, in one branch; the other band is what is left): -6 dB at Freq, 24 dB per octave either side, and the two bands sum flat. ## Added 2026-11-13 (late): Flip warp
- Serum's **Flip** warp repeats with the amount: 0 and 1 are the table as it is, 0.5 is the table read **backwards** (same magnitudes to 0.03 dB, every harmonic's phase negated exactly, at every table position). 0.05 away from 0.5 is already 1.9 dB / 70 degrees off, and 0.15-0.35 is a different, noisy shape (calibration/dev/probe_flip.py).
- The converter therefore carries Flip only within 0.01 of 0 / 1 (nothing to do) or of 0.5 (as the **first** warp of the oscillator): it writes a copy of the wavetable with every frame reversed (sample i takes sample N-i, frame size from the file's `clm ` chunk), named `<table>_flipped.wav` next to the original, and points the oscillator at it. Any other Flip amount, a table without a frame size or a stereo table stays "unsupported".
- Checked end to end on BS - Snapper (octave bands 0.9 dB rms from Serum, level +0.2 dB). Flip at 0.5 changes only phase relations, so it is audible against other oscillators, the sub and nonlinear stages, not on its own.

## Added 2026-11-13 (evening): static oscillator warps, and the comb / flanger voice filters

### Oscillator warps that are a fixed function of the waveform
- **Probe** (`calibration/dev/warpwave.py`, `probe_warp_curves.py`, `probe_evenodd.py`, `probe_warp_phase.py`): a held note of one table with Serum's `kParamRandomPhase` at 0 (the note then starts at the same phase every render, so the cycle can be folded from the known pitch and compared sample for sample; without it the phase is random). A sine table (Basic Shapes) through a warp gives the transfer curve out = f(in) over the whole -1..1 range, a saw (Default Shapes) through the same warp is the cross-check.
- **Memoryless shapers** (the curve predicts the saw to 0.2-7 % rms): DistSoftClip, DistDiode2, DistAsym, DistZeroSquare, DistRectify (a full-wave rectifier at every amount, 0 included), DistSineShaper (up to amount 0.4), Quantize (the identity up to 0.3, followed up to 0.55). They are measured at 41 amounts (0.025 apart) and carried by the engine as a table: `wtN_wshape` 5 ("Curve"), `wtN_sgain` the curve's index, `wtN_sout` Serum's amount over 0..127; `src/dsp/warp_curves.h` is generated by `tools/gen_warp_curves.py` from `calibration/warp_curves_measured.json`, the converter's index is `calibration/warp_curve_modes.json` (modes, the largest amount followed, the amount up to which the curve is the identity). The engine rescales by 1/0.9 at the input and 0.9 at the output because its loader normalises every table to a 0.9 peak and Serum's curves are in file units. Against Serum through the real converter and engine (`calibration/dev/validate_static_warps.py`): 0.02-1.7 dB on the first twelve harmonics (SineShaper 0.4: 2.9, Quantize 0.5: 3.9, which is why they stop there).
- **A second shaper on one oscillator** is now reported (the old check looked for a key that does not exist, `wtN_shape`, so the second shaper silently replaced the first). Techno Loop and Elec Simple Vintage show it.
- **Even/Odd is linear and exact**: out(p) = x(p) + (2 a - 1) x(p + 1/2), on two different tables at every amount 0..1 (error 0.002). 0.5 is the table as it is, 0 keeps only the odd harmonics (doubled), 1 only the even ones (doubled; the same wave an octave up). The converter bakes it into a copy of the table (`<table>_eoNNN.wav`, float32, the other chunks kept) next to the original, so it holds at every table position and for a modulated position, and the old "octave up" stand-in is gone. It may follow filter warps and Flip (they commute with a time shift of half a cycle) but not a FM / PD / RM / Sync / shaper warp. The sum can reach twice the table's peak and the loader normalises a table to its own peak, so the file carries a `tbpk` chunk (the peak of the table it was made from: `WavData::normPeak`, read by `dsp/wt/loader.h` and by `calibration/tablor_cli.cpp`). Against Serum: 0.03 dB.
- **Bend / ASYM**: `PosNeg` is one bipolar knob (`BendPosNeg` 0.25 is `BendPos` 0.5 to the sample, 0.75 is `BendNeg` 0.5, 0.5 the table as it is; same for `ASYMPosNeg`), which `deephouse2move.BIPOLAR_WARPS` now applies (BendPosNeg used to be read as BendPos by its raw amount). `ASYMNeg` is carried like `ASYMPos` was, with the engine's Bend (noted as approximate): ASYM is a phase map that runs past the cycle edge, keeps a saw's harmonics within 0.3 dB and gives a sine a second harmonic at -25 dB at ASYMNeg 0.1; the engine's Bend is 2.7 dB off at ASYMNeg 0.56 and 12 dB off in a -25 dB second harmonic at 0.55.
- **Not carried**: DistSinFold, DistLinFold (the folding is not a function of the waveform the way the others are), Remap 1 / 2 (their amount does nothing on the default table), FM / PD / RM from the noise or Filter 1, Quantize above 0.55, SineShaper above 0.4.

### Comb and flanger voice filters (engine filter types 8..11)
- **The mistake that cost an hour**: Reso is stored in PERCENT (0..100) in the preset, and `H.with_filter(t, cutoff, reso)` writes it as given: a Reso of 0.5 is 0.5 %, and every comb looked like a flat -6 dB. Use 50.
- **Measured** (`calibration/dev/probe_comb.py`: white noise through the filter, Oscillator3 routed to Filter 1; `probe_comb_saw.json`: a held saw, the dB change of 60 harmonics, which is deterministic where the noise is not): the models are feedback combs tuned to the cutoff. With D = sample rate / cutoff, g = +- Reso/100, a = 0.4965 / (1 + |g|):
  - `CombP` / `CombN`: y = a (w + c w[n-D]), w = x + g w[n-D], c = sign g^2.
  - `FlangeP` / `FlangeN`: the same with c = sign (valleys 30+ dB deep).
  fitted to the 60 harmonics at Reso 0..95: 0.01-0.12 dB rms. Var does nothing on CombP. The cutoff is midiNoteToHz(12 log2(2697) x Freq) exactly (425.0 Hz at Freq 0.5), with none of the offsets the other filters carry; key tracking pivots at 31.5 as the others.
- **Engine** (`src/dsp/comb.h`, filter types "Comb +", "Comb -", "Flange +", "Flange -"; `flt_cfine` / `flt2_cfine`, appended last so no earlier parameter moves): a stereo delay line with 4-point Lagrange interpolation and the delay ramped across the block. The cutoff pot is whole semitones, far too coarse to tune a comb, so the converter writes the rest in cents to `fltN_cfine` (64 = 0). Native test `testCombFilter` (impulse response against the law, 0.00 dB). Against Serum through the real converter and engine on a saw (`calibration/dev/validate_comb_saw.py`): mean power within 0.1-0.5 dB, per-harmonic rms 0.15-1.3 dB for the combs (the flanges read a few dB rms because their deepest notches sit at -40 dB and below).
- **Phase 12 / 24 / 36 / 48 "P"** (engine types 12..15): the flange law with the delay replaced by a chain of N = 2, 4, 6, 8 first-order all-passes whose corner is 0.511 x the cutoff (the chain alone at Reso 0 fits the 60 harmonics to 0.0-0.3 dB), with the same feedback g = Reso and level a = 0.4965 / (1 + g): `w = x + g A(w)`, `y = a (w + A(w))`. The loop has no delay (an all-pass passes its input by a1), so `PhaserFilter` solves it: w = (x + g r) / (1 - g a1^N), r the chain's output for a zero input. Against Serum's harmonics 0.2-1.5 dB rms up to Reso 60 %, 3-5 dB at 90 %. `Phase*N` (the minus versions) did not fit any form tried (1-3 dB at best, with a different gain and feedback at every setting) and are not carried.
- **Var does nothing** on CombP, FlangeP or Phase24P (0, 50, 100 identical to 0.00 dB).
- **Not carried**: `Comb2` (peaks and valleys both small, a=0.43-0.7 against Reso, not a clean form), `Combs` (stereo, 6 dB rms off any form tried), the `L6` / `H6` / `HL6` comb variants (a filter in the loop), `DistComb`, `Phase*N`, `Phase*H6P`, `FlangePhase`.

## Added 2026-11-13 (night): warps driven by the Noise, Filter 1 and the wave itself

- **Probes**: `calibration/dev/probe_warp_noise.py` (skirt), `probe_noise_warp_law.py` (carrier line, total power, skirt share, averaged over renders: the modulator is random), `probe_warp_filt.py` (Filter 1 as the modulator), `validate_noise_warps.py` (Serum against the engine, six spectral bands), `validate_static_warps.py` with `CASES=selfpd`.
- **They work at Noise level 0.** The Noise only has to exist; its level does not move the effect.
- **FM from the Noise** (`kFM_NOISE`, `kFMX_NOISE`, the two read alike): a real FM by white noise. Total power is kept, the carrier becomes a Lorentzian line whose half width H grows about x3.3 per 0.1 of amount (0.45: 0.05 Hz, 0.5: 0.4, 0.55: 2, 0.6: 12, 0.7: 52, 0.8: 230, 0.9: 4000, 1.0: 30 000 Hz), read from the share of power left within +-1.4 Hz of the carrier. White frequency noise of s.d. d cycles per sample has H = pi d^2 fs. **Nothing audible below 0.45** (carrier within 0.3 dB). Against Serum's six bands: within 1 dB at 0.5, 1-5 dB at 0.6-0.9.
- **PD from the Noise** (`kPD_NOISE`): a phase modulation by white noise (power kept, carrier exp(-s^2)), on from about 0.55; s^2 = 0.06, 0.22, 0.75, 2.9, 3.6, 4.6, 5.0, 6.0, 9.8 at 0.6 .. 1.0 in steps of 0.05. Band shape within 2 dB of Serum; Serum loses 3 dB of total power at 0.75 and above (its anti-aliasing), the engine 1.3.
- **RM from the Noise** (`kRM_NOISE`): (1 - a) x + 0.409 a^2 x n, n white at unit rms (carrier exactly 1 - a; bands within 1 dB).
- **Engine**: warp source 4 "Noise" (`wtN_xs`, `wtN_xs2`), a white Gaussian of unit s.d. made once per block per voice (`FastGauss modNoise`); the depth laws are `noiseFmSdCycles`, `noisePdSigmaCycles` and `kXwarpNoiseRmGain` in `wt_oscillator.h`. AM from the Noise is not measured, not carried.
- **Dead zones** (`deephouse2move.WARP_DEADZONE`): FM / FMX from the Noise up to 0.45, PD from the Noise up to 0.55, FM from Filter 1 up to 0.16 are left out and noted as approximate (the effect there is under -17 dB). FM from Filter 1 is otherwise a feedback loop (the carrier is its own modulator through the filter; the carrier is gone by 0.2) and is not carried; PD from Filter 1 is dead to 0.3.
- **SelfPD**: the phase pushed by the wave's own value, y = x(p + z y), z = 0.62 a^2 cycles of the table's peak (engine warp kind 7, `selfPdCycles`; three fixed-point steps). A sine matches Serum's 2nd harmonic to 0.4 dB up to 0.35; a saw matches the first three harmonics but the engine falls 5-10 dB faster above the 6th, and above 0.35 the saw does not follow at all. Carried to 0.4 (`SELFPD_LIMIT`), above is reported.
- **DLM** is NOT carried: a phase map in two halves (amount 0.5 plays two cycles per cycle; 0.4 and 0.6 are the same, 0.2 and 0.8 are the same), with a step at the half. Not fitted.
- **Census** (851 presets): complete 304 -> 305, approximate 398 -> 416, sound gap 148 -> 129 (19 fewer). Freed: all the SelfPD, FMX_NOISE and low-amount FM_NOISE presets, Geiger Wub (RM_NOISE), Nils Type Beat, Rim, Roses, Crushed Chord ... Some still carry another gap (Gnarly Bass Stack, Membrane Harp, Sinessss, Interesting Sines, Perc Movie Action). ## Formant voice filters (2026-11-19)

- **Serum's Formant ONE / TWO / TWB are sums of parallel band-pass resonators** (ONE five, TWO three, TWB four), each a unit-gain band-pass of fixed bandwidth (TWO: 120 / 199 / 234 Hz; ONE and TWB the same families), fitted to the per-harmonic response of a held saw at nine low notes to 0.1 dB rms (`calibration/formant_grid.json`: centre, bandwidth and gain of every resonator on a Freq 0..1 by Var 0..100 grid, 11 x 9 points). Between grid points the centre is interpolated in its log, the bandwidth and gain linearly (held-out Freq / Var settings: 0.1 to 1.6 dB).
- **Freq is the cutoff note over 12 log2(2697) = 136.77 semitones** (the comb's law), with no filter calibration offset. **Key tracking slides the resonators along the Freq axis** by (note - 35) semitones, as for the other filters (the probe's kParamKeyTrack behaves as on / off). **Reso divides every bandwidth by 1 + 2 Reso** (0 / 50 / 100 % gave 1, 1/2, 1/3: 0.09 dB rms). Var moves the centres roughly linearly (about 2x from 0 to 100 % in TWO); TWB's top resonators sit near 10 kHz at high Var, where the engine's state-variable stage differs from the fitted analog shape (the stage is widened by 2 theta / sin 2 theta to compensate; the highest Var is still 3 to 5 dB off above 5 kHz).
- **Vital's Formant model** (filter model 5, styles 0 and 1) is four state-variable band-passes in parallel with the vowel tables of `filters/formant_filter.cpp` (X / Y bilinear between the corners a-i-u-o or a-o-i-e, Spread pulls the formants towards note 80, Transpose in semitones, Resonance the knob 0.3..1); it ignores the cutoff, resonance and key tracking knobs. The engine's version matches the plugin to 0.5 dB rms on a saw. Style 2 (vocal tract) has no code in Vital's source and plays nothing.
- **Result on factory presets** (Serum vs the engine, band errors over 90 Hz to 11 kHz, `out/formant_ab`): Serilina 21.5 -> 4.5 dB rms, Vocal Circuit 19.1 -> 6.1, Buzzy Vocal Bass 14.3 -> 12.3 (one very quiet band). A formant preset plays 0.6 to 5.9 dB under Serum until its level is refitted.

## Vital attack curve and live filter Blend (0.9.8 work)

- **Vital's attack stage is `powerScale(x, -attack_power)` over 0..1 of the attack time** (`synthesis/modulators/envelope.cpp`; `powerScale(x, p) = (exp(p x) - 1) / (exp(p) - 1)`, a straight line under |p| 0.01), squared into the gain for the amp envelope as the decay is. A negative power starts slowly, a positive one rises at once. The engine's one-pole attack is about +6 on that scale. `AnalogADSR::setAttackPower` draws it (hidden pots `vca_apow`, `flt_apow`, `me1_apow`, `me2_apow`: 0 keeps the analog attack, 1..127 is power (pot - 64) x 20 / 63, 64 a straight line); the converter writes it when the attack is 10 ms or longer. `calibration/vital_attack_probe.py` (amp envelope on a sine, powers -6..+6, attacks 0.13 and 0.66 s): worst error 29 dB before, 0.7 to 3.8 dB after. A note on over a sounding envelope starts at the matching point of the curve.
- **A route into a voice filter's Blend moves it live.** New destinations Filter Blend and Filter 2 Blend (appended: indices 64, 65; a fraction of the knob's 0..2 range, as the Dist Flt Blend); the Blend (Analog, Dirty, Ladder, Diode), Phaser and Comb filters add it to their stored blend each block. An envelope held at 1 routed into Blend, against the plugin (saw, octave harmonics): Analog 10.4 -> 0.9 dB rms, Phaser 12.6 -> 1.7, Comb 3.5 -> 1.6. The old mean fold is skipped when the live route carries it (`VITAL_BLEND_LIVE`); macro and velocity routes are still folded into the stored blend.
- **The Phaser filter is not the Thumpus regression.** The filter alone is within 1 to 2 dB of the plugin at Thumpus's own setting (style 1, blend 0, cutoff note 133.5, resonance 0). The preset stays 8.5 to 12 dB from Vital with filter 2 off as well (its 125 to 250 Hz band 25 dB low, highs 10 to 16 dB hot), so the cause is elsewhere (an oscillator or a route), and a live Blend route changes it by 0.1 dB.
- **`lfo_N_stereo`** (Vital: the LFO runs at `phase +- stereo / 2` on the left and right channel, `synth_lfo.cpp`): moves a destination differently per channel. It touches 6 of 100 local presets (wave frame, spectral morph, tune, level), all oscillator parameters, which the engine renders once for both channels. Not carried; not a cheap fix.

- **Vital's random modulators** (`random_lfo.cpp`): four styles, Perlin (0), Sample and Hold (1), Sine (2), Lorenz (3); a uniform random value in -1..1 drawn for every voice at note on (`polyVoiceNext`), the output (value x 0.5 + 0.5) over a cycle at the rate (`2^random_N_frequency` Hz, or the tempo-synced rate from `random_N_tempo` as for an LFO). Perlin is `perlinInterpolate(last, next, t)` = 2 ((last t)(1 - s) + (next (t - 1)) s) with s = t^2 (3 - 2t); Sine is `last + (next - last)(0.5 + 0.5 sin(2 pi (t / 2 - 1/4)))`; Sample and Hold keeps `last` until the cycle ends; Lorenz is the attractor (a = 10, b = 28, c = 8/3, step min(0.01, rate x 0.5 / sample rate) per sample, output x / 40 + 0.5). The engine runs them as route sources 5 to 8 (`stepRandoms` in `voice.h`, a block at a time; hidden `rn1..4_style / _rate / _tempo`). Against the plugin (saw, Analog low-pass, random route to the cutoff, 12 notes): per-note and within-note centroid spread agree in shape for all four styles (Perlin and Lorenz vary little between notes, Sample and Hold and Sine a lot; the engine's spread is 10 to 30 % wider, in line with its route depth being about 10 % over at a fixed source). Not carried: per-channel random values (`random_N_stereo`), song-position sync (`sync_type`), a keyboard-following rate; baked destinations (spectral morph and distortion amount) stay at the random value's mean.

- **Voice pitch routes (0.9.9 work).** Vital's `voice_transpose` (range +-48 semitones, an LFO or envelope route adds amount x 96 x source) and `voice_tune` (+-1 semitone, amount x 2 x source) move the note that feeds every oscillator, the sample oscillators and the filters' key tracking (`synth_voice_handler.cpp`). The engine's new destinations Voice Pitch (index 66, 48 semitones per unit of the route) and Voice Fine (67, 2 per unit) add to the base note (`voicePitchMod()` in `voice.h`). An envelope held at 1 routed to `voice_transpose` at 0.10 shifts the plugin +9.59 semitones and the engine +9.90 (the amount pot has 127 steps over 48 semitones); `voice_tune` at 0.5 shifts both +0.99. Destination indices are saved in presets: a new one is only ever appended (a `static_assert` after `enum ModDst` pins the shipped ones).
- **Key and Key-in-octave sources (0.9.9 work).** `note` is the (bent, glided) MIDI note / 127 and `note_in_octave` is `mod(note / 12)` (`voice_handler.cpp prepareVoiceValues`); both are fixed for the life of a note. A route through a curve reads `1 - y` of the curve at that value, then the route's amount (checked against the plugin: a step curve on `note` over osc level switches at x = 0.45, i.e. note 57 / 58, and on `note_in_octave` by pitch class). The engine has no per-route curve, so a Key route becomes an LFO in mode 3 (Key) or 4 (Key Oct): it looks the note up in its Hi-Res table, point note / 2 (0..63) or the pitch class (0..11), so the table is the route's curve (and its power) sampled over the note. Routes with the same source and curve share an LFO; the units are ranked with the real LFOs by the strength of their routes and the rest are reported ("key routes past the engine's four LFOs"). A bipolar route is stored as the engine's bipolar flag with half the amount, as for the LFOs. Not carried: a route that scales another route by the key (Touch Tone's gating, meta-modulation), `note` into a control the engine cannot route (portamento time, LFO frequency, effect centres).
- **Velocity and Aftertouch sources (0.9.9).** Engine route sources 9 and 10 (appended to the LFO / random list). Velocity is the note's velocity / 127; aftertouch is the channel pressure (0xD0) or a pad's own (0xA0, the newest pad stands for the channel), reset to 0 at each new note. The converter makes a velocity route live when its control has an engine destination and a slot is free (ranked below LFO routes of the same size); otherwise it is folded at velocity 100 as before. Aftertouch is live or dropped. The Move's pads may not send pressure at all (it can be switched off), in which case the source stays 0.
- **Comb Blend Transpose live.** `filter_N_blend_transpose` is 0..84 semitones, added to the filter's cutoff note (comb_filter.cpp), and a route adds amount x 84. The engine's destinations Filter Comb Trans (68) and Filter 2 Comb Trans (69) add modOff x 84 to the stored transpose and hold it to 0..84 (only while a route moves it, so a saved preset is unchanged). The stored pot is still limited to 47 semitones (pot 127), so a Vital transpose above that is cut.
- **Key Track off.** `osc_N_midi_track` 0 makes the oscillator's note the constant 48 (`kNoMidiTrackDefault`), whatever is played, and the voice's own transpose does not reach it. The engine's fixed pitch is MIDI note 0 plus the tune, so the converter writes `wtN_fixed` 1 and a tune of 48 + transpose + tune. Touch Tone's two oscillators play its dial tones at 620 and 1214 Hz on every key in the plugin and now in the engine.
- **Measuring Vital against the engine: match the level.** The engine's int16 output has a dither floor near -93 dBFS. At the harness's default volume (20) the engine is about 30 dB under the plugin, so a quiet or dark preset shows that floor as a flat band from 6 to 16 kHz (Moog Pluck read 24.6 dB error, 14.0 dB after matching; the mean over 100 presets fell from 11.8 to 11.0). Move the volume pot until the level over the first 0.5 s matches, and score only the windows where the plugin is within 35 dB of its peak.

## Serum comb / flange / phaser Filter FX (2026-11-20)

- **The Filter FX models CombP, CombN, FlangeP, FlangeN, Phase12P, Phase24P, Phase36P and Phase48P are the voice filter's laws** (comb.h). `calibration/dev/probe_fxfilter_comb.py` runs white noise through one Filter FX of each model at Freq 0.4 / 0.55 / 0.7 and Reso 0 / 50 / 80 and compares the 1/12-octave power ratio with the analytic |H|^2 of the voice law at the same cutoff: comb and flange 0.7 to 0.9 dB rms (the measurement's own noise) with a 0.2 dB level offset; phasers the same at Reso 0 and 2 to 5 dB at Reso 50 to 80, which is the voice fit's own limit. The Phase *N* models are not carried.
- **Drive on these models is a plain gain** (`probe_fxfilter_comb_drive.py`): +9.2 dB at 30 %, +16.3 at 60, +24.1 at 100, the same at a low input level, the shape moving by 0.7 to 1 dB rms. The engine's `serumModelGain` ramps it across the block.
- **Engine:** the Filter FX Model switch (`fxf_vsk`) takes 7..14 (Serum Comb +, Comb -, Flange +, Flange -, Phase 12 / 24 / 36 / 48); `fxf_cfine` (64 = 0) is the cents remainder of the cutoff note, as the voice filter's `flt_cfine`. The converter (`fxmap.dynamic_filter`, `apply_filter2`) sets the knob's note exactly, the Reso as percent / 100, the Drive as percent / 100, and takes an envelope or LFO on the Freq like any Filter FX. Only the main rack has a model; a bus rack's comb stays flagged.
- **A model the engine has no curve for is not a blocker if the node is dry at the saved position** (Wet 0 with nothing but macro / velocity / note routes on it, which are folded at their saved value): `filter_bands` now checks that before it flags the model. - **L6 / H6 / HL6 comb and flange voice filters (2026-11-20).** The comb with a one-pole filter in its loop, measured on a held saw (calibration/dev/probe_comb_loop_saw.py, validate_comb_loop.py): L6 a low-pass (1-p)/(1-p z^-1) with the loop 0.5 sample longer (0 once open), H6 a high-pass (1+p)/2 (1-z^-1)/(1-p z^-1); for both the pole p is one function of x = Var % + 50 x the Freq knob (0.998 at x 50, 0.94 at 75, 0.775 at 85, 0.295 at 95, then the low-pass settles near 0.15 and the high-pass opens), comb.h loopPole. - **Spectral oscillators: imported only when they hardly scan (2026-11-20).** The engine plays a Spectral oscillator's sample like a sampler; Serum resynthesises it from its spectrum, scanning through the file at Scan Rate, with pitch and scan speed independent and the loop mode applied to the scan, and adds its spectral filter and warps. These 8 are still approximations (the spectral filter is left out and the resynthesis differs from sample playback).

- Voice filter model Tablor does not have (Reverb1, DistComb, Phase48, Combs, ...): by default the preset is blocked.
